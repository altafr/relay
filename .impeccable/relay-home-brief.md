# Relay home workspace

## Scope and mode

Primary target: `app/page.tsx`. Mode: Operate. This redesign replaces the existing visual presentation while preserving the product workflow and content.

## Audience, job, and constraints

The primary operator is presenting or using Relay under time pressure. They need to enter a topic, URL, or pasted text; understand which agents are working; inspect three channel outputs; and download Frame's LinkedIn banner. Preserve every functional state, source-mode draft persistence, model status, and responsive behavior. Avoid marketing-page scaffolding, fake navigation, fake settings, decorative dashboards, and visual noise.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Relay is one calm creative workspace where the source remains the center of gravity. Refuse the category-default sidebar dashboard and oversized marketing hero.

OWN-WORLD: Bright cool-white canvas, graphite type, mist-gray grouping surfaces, and a restrained cobalt selection color. System typography with optical sizing; thin separators and functional translucency only in the floating top bar. Controls use familiar Apple-like geometry, 12–16px radii, immediate pressed feedback, and no decorative glass stacking.

STORY: The operator arrives, understands the one action immediately, selects the source type, enters material, and starts the team. A compact five-agent activity strip explains the handoff without competing with the source. Completed posts and the monochrome banner become the dominant artifact and are easy to switch, copy, regenerate, and download.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim floating top bar contains Relay, run status, and a demo label. Below it, a concise title and one integrated source composer occupy the visual center. The five-agent strip sits directly underneath as a single connected sequence, not five equal cards. Results replace empty vertical space after generation. Primary action is anchored inside the composer at lower right.

FORM: User-pinned minimal Apple-style Operate surface; code-first session. Signature move: the connected agent strip transfers a single cobalt activity indicator from specialist to specialist, then resolves into a quiet success state. Seed key: `user-pinned-apple-minimal`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
